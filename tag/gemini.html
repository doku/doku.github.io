Introducing My AI-Powered Tour Guide

Published: Sun 14 December 2025
By Jason Wang

In Projects.

tags: AI Gemini Travel Tour Guide

I’m excited to announce a new project I’ve been working on: an AI-assisted tour guide app!

This app uses the power of Large Language Models (LLMs) to provide a rich, interactive, and personalized touring experience.

Key Features:

  • Landmark Discovery: The app searches for landmarks around your area of interest.
  • Local Events and History: It researches local events, historical facts, and interesting tidbits to make your tour unique.
  • Guided Introductions: Each landmark comes with a guided introduction and a picture.
  • Audio Narration: The introductions are provided as audio, so you can listen as you explore.
