About Me

I am a Cybernetic Experimentalist and Systems Thinker dedicated to exploring the intersection of data, artificial intelligence, and human curiosity.

Since graduating from UC Berkeley with a degree in Data Science in 2019, I have focused on building mental models that help frame the complexities of our rapidly evolving world. My journey is one of continuous learning and experimentation—a practice rooted in Intellectual Humility and a commitment to being an active Listener.

As an AGI Enforcer and Mental Model Framer, I strive to push the boundaries of what is possible with technology while maintaining a deep respect for the systems that govern our existence. Egresspathy is the digital manifestation of this journey—a sanctuary for sharing insights, documenting experiments, and inviting you to join the conversation.
